Delete Your Bharat AI Account

You can request deletion of your Bharat AI account and associated account data.

How to Delete Your Account

Open the Bharat AI app and go to:

Settings → Delete Account

Follow the confirmation steps to permanently delete your account.

What happens after deletion?

Your Bharat AI account and associated personal account information will be deleted from our systems, subject to information that may need to be retained where required by law.

Need Help?

If you cannot access your account, please contact the Bharat AI support team through the support contact provided in the app.

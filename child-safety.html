Bharat AI — Child Safety Standards

Last updated: August 23, 2026

1. Our Commitment to Child Safety

Bharat AI is committed to maintaining a safe environment for all users and to preventing child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE) on the platform.

Bharat AI does not permit child sexual abuse material (CSAM), sexual exploitation of children, grooming, or any other content or behavior that exploits or endangers children.

2. Prohibited Content and Conduct

Bharat AI strictly prohibits:

  • Child sexual abuse material (CSAM)
  • Sexual exploitation or sexualization of children
  • Grooming or attempts to establish inappropriate sexual relationships with minors
  • Requests for, sharing of, or distribution of CSAM
  • Content that facilitates or promotes the sexual exploitation of children
  • Attempts to use Bharat AI to create, distribute, or obtain CSAM
  • Any other activity involving the sexual abuse or exploitation of children

3. Reporting Child Safety Concerns

Users can report child-safety concerns through the reporting mechanism available within the Bharat AI app.

Reports involving suspected child sexual abuse or exploitation are treated seriously and may be reviewed and acted upon in accordance with applicable laws and our safety procedures.

Users should provide enough information for the safety team to understand and investigate the concern.

4. Safety Measures

Bharat AI works to prevent misuse of its AI features for harmful or exploitative purposes.

When prohibited content or behavior is identified, appropriate action may include:

  • Restricting or removing prohibited content
  • Limiting access to relevant features
  • Suspending or restricting accounts
  • Taking other appropriate safety measures permitted by applicable policies and law

5. Cooperation With Authorities

Bharat AI will comply with applicable child-safety laws and legal requirements.

Where required by applicable law, valid legal process, or an appropriate authority, relevant information may be preserved or disclosed to the appropriate authorities.

7. Scope

These Child Safety Standards apply to Bharat AI and its features that allow users to interact with AI-generated or user-provided content.

Bharat AI may update these standards when necessary to improve child safety, comply with applicable laws, or reflect changes to the service.

8. Effective Date

These Child Safety Standards are effective as of August 23, 2026.

Bharat AI — Child Safety Standards

Last updated: August 23, 2026

1. Our Commitment to Child Safety

Bharat AI is committed to maintaining a safe environment for all users and to preventing child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE) on the platform.

Bharat AI does not permit child sexual abuse material (CSAM), sexual exploitation of children, grooming, or any other content or behavior that exploits or endangers children.

2. Prohibited Content and Conduct

Bharat AI strictly prohibits content and behavior involving the sexual exploitation or abuse of children, including:

  • Child sexual abuse material (CSAM).
  • Sexual exploitation or abuse of minors.
  • Grooming or attempts to establish inappropriate sexual contact with minors.
  • Sexualization of children or minors.
  • Requests for, sharing of, or distribution of CSAM.
  • Content that encourages or facilitates the exploitation of children.
  • Any other activity that violates applicable child-safety laws.

3. Reporting Child Safety Concerns

Users can report child-safety concerns by contacting the Bharat AI child-safety team using the email address provided in the Contact section of this page.

Reports involving suspected child sexual abuse or exploitation are treated seriously and may be reviewed and acted upon in accordance with applicable laws and our safety procedures.

Users should provide enough information for the safety team to understand and investigate the concern.

4. Handling of Reports

Bharat AI may review reports involving suspected violations of its child-safety standards and take appropriate action based on the nature and severity of the reported concern.

Appropriate actions may include removing violating content, restricting or suspending accounts, and taking other measures permitted by applicable laws and Bharat AI's safety procedures.

5. Cooperation With Authorities

Bharat AI may cooperate with appropriate law-enforcement or other authorized authorities when required by applicable law or when necessary to address serious child-safety concerns.

6. Prevention and Safety Measures

Bharat AI uses reasonable safety measures designed to help prevent, identify, and respond to prohibited child-safety content and behavior.

Safety measures may include automated systems, user reports, moderation processes, account restrictions, and other appropriate safeguards.

7. Age and User Safety

Users are expected to comply with the applicable age requirements and terms of service for Bharat AI and the platform through which Bharat AI is provided.

Bharat AI does not allow the platform to be used for the exploitation, abuse, or endangerment of children.

8. Contact

9. Updates to These Standards

Bharat AI may update these Child Safety Standards from time to time to reflect changes in our safety practices, applicable laws, or platform requirements.

The latest version will be published on this page with the applicable update date.
